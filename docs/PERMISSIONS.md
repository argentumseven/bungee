# Phone permissions and optional access

Bungee runs as an ordinary removable Android 12+ app, including on GrapheneOS. Pairing a phone does **not** grant Android permissions or turn on recovery features. Enable only what you intend to use. Names of Android Settings screens vary by OS version; most app grants are under **Android Settings → Apps → Bungee → Permissions**. Bungee's own choices are under **Sharing and permissions** on the phone's main screen.

## Pairing and normal GPS tracking

1. Install the signed Bungee APK, open the app and pair it using an invitation created on your server for this phone's displayed public key. Tap **Start sharing** while Bungee is visible. Bungee requests Android **Location** and, on Android 13+, **Notifications**.
2. For GPS history, grant **precise location** (not approximate-only) and **Allow only while using the app** for the initial visible start. Bungee reads Android's `GPS_PROVIDER`, not Google Play location services or network-based positioning. Enable the device's main Location control. GPS history also requires Bungee's separate **Share GPS history** switch. **Connected to server** alone does not mean a valid GPS fix has been collected.
3. Grant **notifications** if you want the foreground sharing/camera status to be visible normally and want recovery/lost-mode messages to appear. Android 13+ asks for `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`; older releases use Android's app notification settings without that runtime request. Keep Bungee's sharing, recovery-message and camera-protection channels enabled as applicable. The OS controls whether a message can appear or sound.
4. For GPS to resume automatically **after a reboot/first unlock or other background service restart**, open **Android Settings → Apps → Bungee → Permissions → Location**, select **Allow all the time**, and keep **Use precise location** on. Then approve Bungee's **Resume after restart** option. In the app, **Allow GPS after restart** opens the relevant settings guidance; background access is not granted by the normal first location prompt. Without background location, a connection may resume while GPS remains paused; opening Bungee and starting/resuming GPS from its visible screen still works. No location is available from Bungee before the first unlock after reboot.
5. Verify the phone reports a **dated GPS observation**, not merely a connection. Test cellular access to your numeric WireGuard endpoint, and test background/reboot behavior on the actual phone. Android battery limits, force-stop, disabled Location, device lock state, privacy controls and GNSS availability can still interrupt collection. Bungee does not grant itself immunity from OS battery policy.

## Optional recovery functions

| Feature | What to grant or approve | What it enables and does not enable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Household commands | In Bungee, enable **Household commands** and approve with the existing phone lock. | Allows the server to request alarms, recovery messages and temporary GPS fixes (subject to their own permissions). Commands are off until you opt in. It does not grant new Android privileges. |
| Remote screen lock / eligible failed-PIN detection | Set a secure Android PIN/password, then turn on Bungee's **Removable device administration** and confirm Android's administrator prompt. | Grants ordinary **force-lock** and **watch-login** policy uses only. It is removable in Android Settings; this is **not** device-owner/profile-owner provisioning and does not grant wipe, reboot or credential-reset controls. |
| Theft photos and manual pictures | Grant **Camera** permission, allow notifications, enable sharing and household commands, activate removable device administration, then select **Enable theft photos** in Bungee while unlocked. | The locally armed foreground camera service can attempt a front-camera capture after **three new eligible failed PIN/password entries**. A ready service can also receive a manual picture request. Biometric failures may not count. Privacy switch, hardware, OS restrictions and rate limits still apply. The server cannot remotely arm it. |
| Flash with an alarm | Grant **Camera** and enable household commands, if you want torch flash. | The ordinary sound-only alarm does not require camera access. Flash requires suitable rear-camera flash hardware and availability. |
| Automatic theft-photo recovery after reboot/app update | After theft photos are enabled, choose **Enable automatic boot recovery**, authenticate, and grant Android **Display over other apps** to Bungee when its settings page opens. | This optional, broad special access permits Bungee to launch a brief visible recovery screen **after first unlock**; it does not draw an overlay or replace the lock screen. If omitted/revoked, open Bungee while unlocked to resume protection. A force-stop still requires manual launch. |
| In-app APK installation | Only when installing an update from within Bungee: permit **Install unknown apps / Allow from this source** when Android requests it. | Lets Android open the verified APK installer. Android still requests your confirmation; no unattended phone APK upgrade. Not required for ordinary tracking. |

To disable optional access, turn its Bungee switch off or revoke the Android permission. To stop Bungee without the server, use **Pairing & recovery → Stop Bungee**, or Android's own app controls. Already uploaded location history or photos must be erased separately on the server. Read [recovery and camera details](RECOVERY_CONTROLS.md) and [local stop/removal limits](SAFETY.md).

## What you do **not** need to grant

No root, ADB, Accessibility service, device-owner or profile-owner enrollment, Android VPN permission, system-wide DNS control or silent installation privilege is required. Bungee's WireGuard transport is **inside the app** and does not consume Android's system VPN slot. You should **not** grant `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS` to a new installation; the manifest retains its declaration only to undo settings for old installations. Ordinary pairing, GPS and alarms do not require device administration or Display over other apps.

Android's file-based encryption and a strong screen-lock credential are recommended independently of these app permissions. See [the safety rationale for excluding remote wipe](SAFETY.md#controls-excluded-from-the-product).
